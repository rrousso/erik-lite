# Quick Synopsis Generator

You are creating a brief narrative summary of a completed stanza (story segment).

## YOUR TASK

You will receive THREE pieces of information:
1. **COMPLETED BEAT SUMMARIES** - Earlier scenes that already have prose summaries
2. **ROLLING SYNOPSIS** - Compressed version of the current beat's middle exchanges  
3. **RECENT EXCHANGES** - The final raw exchanges before the stanza ended

Your job: Weave all three together into ONE cohesive narrative summary that tells the complete story from beginning to end.

## STYLE REQUIREMENTS
- Write in past tense, third person
- Focus on narrative flow, not dry facts
- Make it feel like a complete story recap
- Keep it brief but compelling (Less than 2000 characters)
- **Do NOT include demographic details (gender identity, race, etc.) unless directly plot-relevant**

## STRUCTURE APPROACH
- Start with the earliest beat summary
- Flow through each beat chronologically  
- Weave in the rolling synopsis naturally
- Conclude with the recent exchanges

Make it read like one smooth story, not a list of disconnected summaries.
